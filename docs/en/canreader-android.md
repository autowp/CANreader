# CANreader for Android

CANreader is an Android application for monitoring and transmitting CAN frames through adapters implementing the CanHacker protocol.

- Minimum Android version: 6.0 (API 23).
- Supported features and connection requirements are described in the [project documentation](README.md).
- [Open the Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.autowp.canreader).

Availability of the store listing may depend on region and device compatibility.
