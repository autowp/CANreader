# Disclaimer and safety

CANreader is provided for development, education, and authorized diagnostic work. Hardware compatibility, firmware behavior, and CAN-bus interpretation depend on the connected equipment and vehicle; the project documentation cannot verify a particular installation.

Connecting to or transmitting on a vehicle CAN bus can affect safety-critical systems, damage equipment, or cause unintended vehicle behavior. Use only a correctly identified bus, follow the vehicle and adapter manufacturers' instructions, and do not transmit while driving. Do not connect to safety-critical networks unless you are qualified and authorized to do so.

Start with a non-vehicle test setup (for example, the app's loopback connection or a bench CAN network). The software is provided without guarantees that it is fit for a particular purpose; you are responsible for assessing risk and verifying every connection and transmission.
