# Connecting to a vehicle CAN bus

Vehicle CAN wiring, bit rate, and network type vary by make, model, year, and bus. Consult the manufacturer's service information and a qualified technician to identify the correct bus and connection points. Never infer wiring or bitrate from another vehicle.

Use an isolated bench setup whenever possible. If vehicle access is necessary, connect only with the vehicle safely parked, use an adapter and wiring rated for the installation, and begin in receive/monitor mode. Transmitting arbitrary frames can disrupt braking, steering, powertrain, airbag, or other safety-related systems.

Do not connect or operate CANreader while driving. Do not modify, terminate, probe, or transmit on a vehicle network unless you understand the electrical requirements and are authorized to do so.
